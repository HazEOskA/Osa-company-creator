# ADR-0005: OBTP is provider- and transport-neutral

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
Open Business Transition Protocol defines authority, execution, evidence, verification, economics and transition semantics independently of provider and transport.

## Consequence
Google Labs, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, MCP, A2A, HTTP, queues and future execution systems can act as replaceable integrations behind a stable business-transition contract.
